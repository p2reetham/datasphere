Department mission

Why the department exists

The Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science is built to turn curious students into confident practitioners — grounding classroom theory in real datasets, real problems, and real tools. We aim to give every student a hands-on path through machine learning, statistics, and software engineering, so they graduate ready to build, not just to describe.

About the club

Where the department's students build together

Data Sphere is the student-run club of the AI & DS department. We run weekly build sessions, dataset deep-dives, and short project sprints where members ship something small every time — a model, a dashboard, a scraper, a visualization.

Beyond the weekly sessions, the club organizes hackathons, guest talks from practitioners, and peer-led workshops. No prior experience is required — just a willingness to open a notebook and try something.

What members get

→ Hands-on project sprints, every week

→ Access to compute and datasets

→ Mentorship from seniors and faculty

→ A team for hackathons and competitions
