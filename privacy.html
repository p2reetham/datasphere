Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Data Sphere Club, AI & DS Department, Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Bengaluru

1. Overview

Data Sphere Club ("the Club", "we", "us") is a student-run club of the AI & DS Department at Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and club activities, and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

  • Registration details — name, email, phone number, department/year, and anything else you submit through our Google Form when you register.
  • Feedback — anything you submit through the feedback form linked on this site.
  • Event photos and videos — taken at club events and workshops, some of which may be published in the Gallery on this site.
  • Theme preference — this site remembers your chosen color theme in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Leaderboard — if the Club runs a members' leaderboard, your name and points may be shown publicly on the Leaderboard page. Ask us if you'd prefer initials, or to be left off.

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use registration details to manage club membership, share updates about meetings and events, and coordinate activities. Photos and videos from events may be used in the Gallery on this site and in the department's own promotional material.

4. Sharing Your Information

We don't sell or rent your information. It's only shared with club coordinators and the faculty advisor for the purpose of running the club, or where required by the institution.

5. Third-Party Services

Registration and feedback are collected through Google Forms, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy. This site is hosted on Vercel, with its source code and content stored on GitHub.

6. Photos and Videos

By attending a Data Sphere Club event, you may be photographed or filmed for the Gallery on this site. If you'd like a specific photo or video removed, contact us using the details below and we'll take it down.

7. Data Retention

We keep registration information for as long as it's useful for running the club — generally for the academic year(s) you're an active member. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

8. Your Choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by reaching out to a club coordinator or through the feedback link on this site.

9. Children's Privacy

This site and the Club are intended for college students and are not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the feedback link on the home page, or directly to a Data Sphere Club coordinator.
